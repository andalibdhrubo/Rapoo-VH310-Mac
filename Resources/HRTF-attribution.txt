Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, MIT Media Lab, 1994.

on use provided the authors are cited for research/commercial applications.
Source: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html
Archive: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR/compact.zip
Included measurement: elev0/H0e030a.wav (128 samples, stereo, 44.1 kHz).
Channel 1 is the left ear (far), channel 2 the right ear (near) for a source
30 degrees right. Ear responses are swapped for a mirrored source 30 degrees
left. Both speaker sources stay horizontal and in front; no head tracking.
Rates 48, 88.2 and 96 kHz resampled with scipy.signal.resample_poly, with
coefficient sum scaling by originalRate/newRate to retain impulse gain.
Processing is an independent implementation, not Rapoo's original algorithm.

FINAL 1.0 SURROUND ADDITIONS
The 7.1 renderer uses horizontal compact measurements from elev0/H0e000a.wav
through H0e180a.wav in 5-degree steps; negative angles swap ear responses.
Default directions: FL -30, FR +30, C 0, BL -150, BR +150, SL -90, SR +90.
LFE is a centered two-pole 120 Hz lowpass, not a directional HRIR.
Rotation selects the nearest 5-degree measurement, with 30 ms filter crossfade.
All coefficient banks and a source/hash manifest are included. Original 0, 30,
90 and 150 degree WAVs are supplied for reproducible isolated-channel tests.
Stereo upmix derives ambience from L-R with a 12 ms rear delay. It cannot
recover independent surround channels lost during upstream stereo downmix.
